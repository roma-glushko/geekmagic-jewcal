# The screens

The **main screen** stays up most of the time. Every few minutes, JewCal shows each other screen that matters right
now, once, and then returns to the main screen. Holiday screens appear about a week before their holiday. The timing
is in [Settings](settings.md#display).

From top to bottom:

**The clock and the dates.** The civil date and the Hebrew date. The Hebrew date turns over at sunset, and a small
moon marks the evening. When weather is available, the temperature and a small icon sit on the right.

**The sky.** It follows the real sun at your location: blue by day, purple and orange at dusk, starry at night.

**The big card** is about whatever is current or next:

- **Before Shabbat or Yom Tov:** its name (or the week's parsha), when to light candles, and a countdown. A badge
  marks a run of several days, such as **3 DAYS**. Notes appear when they apply: *eruv tavshilin today*, or *light
  the Chanukah candles first*.
- **During Shabbat or Yom Tov:** the greeting, which day it is (**DAY 2/3**), and the next evening with its time:
    - a candle for candle lighting before sunset;
    - a star (★) for candles lit after nightfall, from an existing flame;
    - the havdalah candle at the end.
- **During a fast, or within a day of one:** when it begins and ends. A fast on Friday ends with Shabbat.

**NEXT** names the next holiday, fast or Rosh Chodesh that the card does not show, with the days until it.

**The season ribbon** runs along the bottom in its season:

- Elul, with the days to Rosh Hashana.
- The Ten Days of Return.
- The Omer: the day and weeks, and after sunset, *tonight's* count.
- The Three Weeks, and the Nine Days with the days to Tisha B'Av.

!!! note "A small red pixel in the corner"
    It means the calendar has not been refreshed for more than 36 hours, usually because hebcal.com could not be
    reached. The times shown are still right; JewCal keeps retrying.

## Shabbat and Yom Tov, evening by evening

<div class="screens" markdown>
<figure markdown="span">
  ![Three days of Yom Tov and Shabbat](screens/erev-pesach-3day-period.png){ width="240" }
  <figcaption>Pesach on Thursday and Friday, then Shabbat</figcaption>
</figure>
</div>

When two days of Yom Tov run into Shabbat, this screen lays out each evening with its weekday, its kind and its time:

- the first candles before sunset;
- the second night's candles after nightfall, from an existing flame;
- Friday's candles before sunset again;
- havdalah only at the very end.

Notes such as *eruv tavshilin on Wednesday* sit underneath. For a plain Shabbat it shows the parsha, any special
Shabbat, candles and havdalah.

## Messages

| On screen | Means |
|---|---|
| **SETUP** | JewCal needs your WiFi: see *Connect to your WiFi* in the install guide for your model |
| **CONNECTING** | Joining your WiFi network after starting up |
| **SYNCING TIME** | Waiting for the internet clock after starting up |
| **SET LOCATION** | Open the settings page and choose your city |
| **LOADING** | Fetching the calendar from hebcal.com |
| **NO CALENDAR** | hebcal.com could not be reached; JewCal retries by itself (see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md)) |
| **SAFE MODE** (Ultra) | JewCal crashed three times in a row; see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md#safe-mode) |
